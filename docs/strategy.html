Metric & strategy — what the math rewards

Source of truth: problem description (metric + submission format), forum 11516 (masking), forum 11536 (new-fault definition). Transcribed into scripts/dti_metric.py.

1 · The distance-weighted Tversky index (DTI), exactly

Predictions are per-pixel probabilities p(x) ∈ [0,1]; ground truth g(x) ∈ {0,1} marks new-fault pixels. With triangular kernel k(d) = max(1 − d/R, 0), R = 300 m (3 px at 100 m):

TPw = Σ_{g∈G} max_{x:d(x,g)≤R} p(x)·k(d(x,g))        (best kernel-weighted hit per truth pixel)
FPw = Σ_{x:p(x)>0} p(x)·[1 − max_{g∈G} k(d(x,g))]   (mass far from truth penalised ~fully)
FNw = Σ_{g∈G} [1 − max_{x:d(x,g)≤R} p(x)·k(d(x,g))]  (truth pixels with no nearby mass)
DTI = TPw / (TPw + α·FPw + β·FNw + ε),   α = 0.2, β = 0.8

Worked example from the official page: TPw=3.00, FPw=1.89, FNw=2.00 → DTI = 0.60. Our dti_metric.py reproduces 0.60 on that fixture before any other use.

2 · Five strategic consequences

#ConsequenceDesign rule
S1Recall dominates. β/α = 4: a missed fault pixel costs 4× a false-alarm pixel. Thin, high-confidence traces beat wide uncertain blobs only up to a point — sibling measurement shows smaller budgets beat larger ones at matched mass, i.e. precision still binds through the budget.Exact top-k binary emission; tune k on the holdout, never by feel.
S2Near-misses are cheap. FP mass within 300 m of truth pays ≈0; truth within 300 m of mass counts ≈a hit. The kernel forgives ~3 px of mislocation.1-px skeletonised traces are sufficient; do not spend budget on width (sibling width curve peaks at 0 px).
S3Known faults are free. USGS/INGENIOUS pixels are masked from scoring in both rounds (staff, forum 11516). Emitting the catalogue costs nothing and loses nothing.Union every candidate with the catalogue (zero-cost floor); never spend budget re-finding it.
S4Geometry of known systems counts. "New fault" includes newly mapped extensions/splays/strands of existing systems (staff, forum 11536).Weight search density along-strike from tips + inside stepovers/zones (hypotheses E1, E2) above blank far-field ground.
S5Phase 2 re-scores everything. The same file is rescored against expert-expanded labels built partly from our predictions; the final round holds $250k of $300k.Predict what we truly believe are faults (mechanistically defensible traces experts can verify), not metric-gamed confetti. Reviewable traces > lucky pixels.

3 · What beats 0.3168

The leader (DARD, 0.3168 at 2026-09-30 fetch) is ~2× our group's best reported file (0.1855) and ~2× the duplicated 0.1563 pattern. Under S1–S4, the gap is almost certainly recalled unmapped traces, not better calibration: with β=0.8, each newly recalled fault strand inside the kernel buys roughly 4× what an equivalent false-alarm clean-out saves. Our path is therefore:

  1. E1 first: depth-gated buried-fault lineaments — the population a surface catalogue structurally misses.
  2. E2 alongside: kinematic splay fans at tips/stepovers — the population the scoring definition explicitly rewards.
  3. E3/E4 as independent witnesses: fluvial alignments and hydrothermal alignments confirm or veto E1/E2 traces by independent physics; conjunctions enter the emission, singletons stay cheap.
  4. E5 as budget hygiene: evict berms/road-cuts/shorelines so the top-k budget spends on recall.

What we will not do: re-upload the 0.1563 bytes (proven ceiling, not a floor to build on); train another generic ridge blender and hope; spend slots on unvalidated ideas.

4 · Budget arithmetic (verify locally before trusting)

Sibling census (re-measure, don't trust): footprint 5,167,373 px; known faults ~60,988 px (~1.18%). The 0.1563 artifact emits 172,974 px (~3.3%); the 16GEMSDOE candidates emit ~2.4%.
