What is actually scored

Both prize rounds score new expert-mapped faults absent from USGS/INGENIOUS — and staff confirmed known-fault pixels are masked/excluded from scoring (forum 11516), while "new fault" explicitly includes newly mapped geometry of an existing fault system — extensions, splays, parallel strands (forum 11536). Reproducing the catalogue therefore earns nothing by itself; finding unmapped traces — especially near known systems — is the entire game. The metric (α=0.2, β=0.8, R=300 m) punishes missed faults 4× harder than false alarms: it is a recall game under a pixel budget. Details: Metric & strategy.
